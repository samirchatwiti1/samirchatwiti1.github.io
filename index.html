HourCalendar

Hour-by-hour calendar for your monday.com boards — subitems included.

HourCalendar turns any monday.com board into a true hourly calendar: start and end times, 30-minute slots, day / week / month views, and drag-and-drop scheduling that writes real times back to your date columns.

  • Subitems appear as first-class events, labeled with their parent item.
  • Drag items from the "Unscheduled" panel straight onto the calendar.
  • Create items by clicking any empty slot.
  • 100% client-side: your data never leaves monday.com.
