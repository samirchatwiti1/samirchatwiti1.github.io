HourCalendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

HourCalendar ("the App") is a board view application for monday.com.

What data the App accesses

The App reads the following data from your monday.com account, strictly to display and update your calendar:

  • Board structure: columns, groups, and their titles.
  • Items and subitems: names, date columns, and the columns you choose to display (for example a Status column).

The App writes to your monday.com account only the changes you explicitly make in the calendar: updating a date or duration column when you drag, drop, or resize an event, and creating items when you use the "New item" dialog.

What data the App stores

The App has no backend and no database. It runs entirely in your browser, inside monday.com.

  • Your board data never leaves monday.com: the App communicates exclusively with the monday.com API, under the permissions (scopes) you approved at installation (boards:read, boards:write).
  • The App stores only its own settings (which columns you mapped, display preferences) using monday's storage API, inside your monday.com account.
  • The App uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, and sends nothing to any third-party server.

Data sharing

We do not collect, sell, share, or transfer any of your data. We technically cannot: the App has no server of its own.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App removes its access to your account. Settings stored via monday's storage API are managed by monday.com and tied to your account.
